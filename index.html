Petal Link

Link, bouquet, code

Hand it a link. Watch the bouquet fold itself into a code you can scan.

Ready to scan

  1. Type or paste any link. You can leave off the https://.
  2. Every petal and bud flies to a spot in the code, and the gaps fill in with colors from the nearest flower.
  3. Point a phone camera at the finished code. No app needed.
